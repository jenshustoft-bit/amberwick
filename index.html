YouTube Machine

YouTube Machine is the private production tool behind Amberwick. It runs on the owner's own computer and is used only by the owner, for Amberwick and the owner's other YouTube channels. With the owner's permission, it uploads those channels' finished videos to YouTube, sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and release times, and reads basic information about the owner's own channels.

It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.
